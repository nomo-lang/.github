# Nomo Community Code of Conduct

## Our standard

The Nomo community is committed to a focused, welcoming, and technically
rigorous environment. We expect participants to:

- discuss ideas and code without attacking people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- welcome questions and different levels of experience;
- respect stated boundaries and private information;
- credit the work and ideas of others;
- accept maintainers' decisions about scope while remaining free to disagree
  constructively.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
misgendering, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private
communications without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in Nomo repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, community events, and public spaces where someone is
representing the Nomo project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in public issues. Ask a maintainer
for a private reporting channel without including private details in the
request. Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to review the
report, consider context and impact, and may remove content, issue a warning,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude a participant.

Retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern is itself a
violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by established open-source community codes of conduct
and is maintained as part of the Nomo governance documentation.
